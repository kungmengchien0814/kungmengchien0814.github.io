Research

In Prof. Peng-Jen Chen's group since 2024, with technical and equipment support from ITRI's Green Energy and Environment Research Laboratories. I presented all seven papers.

Conference papers

  • 2026 Flow-Field Optimization of an Anion-Exchange-Membrane Water Electrolyzer Cell 12th Asian Conference on Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning (ACRA 2026), international Adding an outlet diffuser lowered pressure drop by about 34% against the baseline and raised near-electrode velocity. An output of NSTC project 114-2637-E-167-015. First author, English oral presentation
  • 2025 Flow-Field and Thermal Characteristics of Single-Cell AEM Electrolyzer ILASS-Asia 2025, National Cheng Kung University, international A nickel mesh on the anode made the flow more uniform and raised mean velocity by about 22%, at about 17 times the pressure drop. First author, English oral presentation
  • 2025 Flow-Channel Design and Heat-Transfer Numerical Analysis of an AEM Electrolyzer HEFC 2025 International Hydrogen Energy and Fuel Cell Conference, in Chinese A single-cell model coupling flow, conjugate heat transfer and OH⁻ transport; main-channel velocity uniformity about 93% at 0.1 m/s inlet velocity. Corresponding author, oral presentation
  • 2025 Structural Parameter Optimization of an AEM Hydrogen Electrolyzer 10th ISME National Conference (ISME 2025), National Cheng Kung University, in Chinese The single-cell model extended to twelve cells in series: velocity dropped by about 40% in the downstream cells, with hot spots in cells 3–10. First author, oral presentation
  • 2024 Structural Parameter Optimization of an AEM Hydrogen Electrolyzer 41st National Conference of the Chinese Society of Mechanical Engineers (CSME 2024), in Chinese Inlet diffuser angle, inlet flow rate and mesh thickness optimized together with response surface methodology and Newton's gradient method; the performance index came out about 20% above the current product parameters. Corresponding author, oral presentation
  • 2024 Numerical Simulation of Near-Membrane Flow Characteristics in an Electrolyzer 2nd ACMT Annual Meeting (ACMT 2024), in Chinese After a mesh-independence study, simulated pressure drop matched ITRI's measurements with a mean error of about 0.006 bar at 4 million elements. Corresponding author, mini-forum oral presentation
  • 2024 Structural Flow-Field Analysis of Electrolytic Hydrogen EquipmentExcellent Paper 19th National Hydrogen Energy and Fuel Cell Conference (HEFC 2024), in Chinese Compared the outlet diffuser, the nickel-mesh layer and inlet diffuser angles by pressure drop and near-electrode velocity; Excellent Paper Award. Corresponding author, oral presentation
